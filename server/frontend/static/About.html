About Us

Meet the team behind Best Cars Dealership

Best Cars Dealership is a full-stack car dealership review platform built as a capstone project. Our small team designed and developed every layer of the application, from the Django REST backend to the React frontend, the Node/MongoDB dealer microservice, and the Flask sentiment analysis service that powers our review insights.
